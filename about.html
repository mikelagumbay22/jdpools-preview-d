About Us

Built Around Your Vision. Focused on the Details.

Get to know the team behind JD Pools & Outdoor Living.

Your backyard, our focus

A pool or outdoor living project is a meaningful investment in your home. Choosing the right company matters.

At JD Pools & Outdoor Living, our focus is on understanding what matters to you, helping shape a clear project plan, and delivering work with care and attention to detail.

Experience & craftsmanship

Who We Are

At JD Pools & Outdoor Living, we believe every backyard has the potential to become something extraordinary. With more than 10 years of experience building pools as subcontractors for leading contractors, and now leading projects directly as JD Pools & Outdoor Living, we bring ideas to life through custom swimming pool construction, remodeling, and quality craftsmanship.

What sets us apart is the value we place on every client and every project. We take the time to listen, understand your vision, and work closely with you throughout the process. By combining experience, attention to detail, and a commitment to doing quality work, we create outdoor spaces that are thoughtfully designed, built to be enjoyed, and uniquely yours.

From ideas to enjoyment

Our Approach

  1. 1

    Initial Consultation – Let's Start with Your Vision.

    Every great project begins with a conversation. We take the time to understand your ideas, discuss your goals, evaluate your space, and explore the possibilities for your backyard.

  2. 2

    Project Planning & Estimate – A Clear Plan for Your Project.

    Once we understand your vision, we discuss design options, materials, features, and project details. We provide an estimate outlining the proposed scope of work so you can move forward with a clear understanding of your project.

  3. 3

    Construction – Bringing Your Vision to Life.

    This is where the transformation begins. Our team works to bring your project together through skilled craftsmanship, attention to detail, and communication throughout the construction process.

  4. 4

    Final Walkthrough – Every Detail Matters.

    As your project comes to completion, we walk through the finished work with you, review the completed scope, and address any remaining questions. Our goal is to make sure you understand your finished project and are ready to enjoy your new outdoor space.
